# TCGA-LUAD

Lung adenocarcinoma primary tumours from The Cancer Genome Atlas: 510 samples, 19,719 genes,
10 clinical columns. `real_data/` holds the real cohort and its split manifest; `dbTwin/` holds
the benchmark settings, report, and saved outputs. The synthetic CSVs are on
[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/NandaAditya/rna-twin).

## Source

cBioPortal study [Lung Adenocarcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)](https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=luad_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018):
`data_mrna_seq_v2_rsem.txt`, `data_clinical_patient.txt`, `data_clinical_sample.txt`, and
`data_mutations.txt`.

## Expression (`real_data/expression.csv.gz`)

- **Values are upper-quartile-normalized RSEM gene estimates, not read counts.** They are
  non-integer, and each sample's 75th percentile of non-zero genes is about 1,000. The synthetic
  TCGA-LUAD values are integers on the same scale. The benchmark uses the values as they are for
  log2(CPM + 1) and rounds them to integers only for DESeq2, as it does for every cohort. DESeq2
  assumes raw counts, so read the TCGA-LUAD DE p-values as a like-for-like comparison of real
  and synthetic data, not as calibrated tests.
- Rows are samples (`TCGA-xx-xxxx-01`), columns are genes. Hugo symbols were mapped to Ensembl
  gene IDs without version suffixes. 2,786 symbols had no match and keep their symbol, some of
  them retired (e.g. `PKM2`, `TMSL3`). Rows without a symbol were dropped, and duplicate symbols
  keep their first row.
- Genes with a total of 10 or less across samples were removed.
- Only samples with both expression and clinical data were kept.

## Clinical columns (`real_data/metadata.csv`)

| Column | From | Values |
|---|---|---|
| `clinical_os_months`, `clinical_os_status` | `OS_MONTHS`, `OS_STATUS` | months; `0:LIVING` / `1:DECEASED` |
| `clinical_age`, `clinical_sex` | `AGE`, `SEX` | years; `Female` / `Male` |
| `clinical_egfr_mut`, `clinical_kras_mut`, `clinical_alk_mut`, `clinical_tp53_mut`, `clinical_stk11_mut` | `data_mutations.txt` | 1 if the sample has any record for that gene, else 0 |
| `clinical_stage_clean` | `AJCC_PATHOLOGIC_TUMOR_STAGE` | `I` (I, IA, IB), `II` (II, IIA, IIB), `advanced` (IIIA, IIIB, IV) |

Mutation flags count every record in the mutation file, including silent and non-coding
variants. Four samples have no mutation records, so all five of their flags are 0. Stage is
missing for two samples, which are left out of the prediction splits.
